\subsection*{Biography}

Aasso Ziro received the Master of Technology degree from Kazakh-British Technical
University (KBTU), Almaty, Kazakhstan.
He is a Senior Lecturer at the School of Information Technology and Engineering, KBTU,
and a Ph.D.\ candidate at the Faculty of Information Technology,
Al-Farabi Kazakh National University (KazNU).
His work covers information security auditing, cybersecurity, and the application of
machine learning in educational and business settings.
He has 7 peer-reviewed publications (9 citations as of 2025), including:

  \item Ziro, A., Gnatyuk, S., \& Toibayeva, S.
    ``Research of the Information Security Audit System in Organizations.''

    ``Improved Method for Penetration Testing of Web Applications.''

\subsection*{Contributions to This Manuscript}

Aasso Ziro validated the domain conventions of the Kazakhstani Unified National
Testing (UNT) system---score-scale interpretation, discipline taxonomy, and the
pedagogical significance of the platform's test-type categories.
This expertise directly underpinned the pseudo-skill cleaning criteria in
Section~3.1 and the per-discipline analysis in Figure~15.
He reviewed and confirmed the per-stratum conformal coverage results (Table~13) and
the per-discipline $R^{2}$ values (Figure~15) against independently maintained
evaluation records.
He reviewed all manuscript drafts for factual accuracy of the Kazakhstani educational
context, verified discipline names and UNT-specific terminology, and contributed to
the revision-letter responses for Reviewer~1 Comments~1 and~2.

\subsection*{Biography}

Ruslan Bulgakov is a researcher at Al-Farabi Kazakh National University (KazNU),
Almaty, Kazakhstan, with expertise in statistical machine learning and
uncertainty quantification.

\subsection*{Contributions to This Manuscript}

Ruslan Bulgakov contributed expertise in statistical measurement and uncertainty
quantification to the design and interpretation of the conformal prediction framework
in Section~3.3.
He advised on the suitability of the Glicko rating deviation as a Mondrian grouping
variable and reviewed the exchangeability assumptions underlying the split-conformal
calibration procedure.
He independently verified the conformal coverage guarantees in Table~13,
cross-checking Wilson confidence interval calculations for all three rating-deviation (RD) bands,
and reviewed the component ablation (Table~12) for consistency with the headline
accuracy metrics.
He provided critical revisions to the Methods (Section~3.3, conformal prediction
subsection) and the Discussion (Section~5.3, Score Ranges), and reviewed the
revision-letter responses to Reviewer~2 Comments~8 and~10.

\section*{Author Change Justification}
%% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We respectfully submit this statement in response to the editorial inquiry regarding
author changes between the original submission and the Round~1 revision.

The revision adds \textbf{Artem Bykov} and \textbf{Aadm Ziro} (both at IITU, Almaty)
as co-authors.
Their names were omitted from the original submission due to an administrative
oversight at the time of manuscript system entry.
Their contributions are:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Artem Bykov} (IITU): Data curation---platform database export and
    schema documentation; Resources---coordination of data access with the platform
    operator; Writing---review and editing.
  \item \textbf{Aadm Ziro} (IITU): Data curation---pseudo-skill field identification
    using platform domain knowledge; Resources; Writing---review and editing.
\end{itemize}

Neither added author has a competing interest with the manuscript content.
All five authors have reviewed and approved the final revised manuscript and agree to
be accountable for all aspects of the work.
No authors have been removed and the order of existing authors is unchanged.
